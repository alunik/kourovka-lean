## Statement and attribution

The [Notebook primary-source indexed entry 21.35](https://alglog.org/21tkt.pdf) again identifies the unrestricted finite-group question and the proposers Y. Contreras Rojas, V. Grazian and C. Monetta. Direct PDF retrieval timed out, so the exact entry was reconfirmed from the indexed primary excerpt alongside the earlier pinned source. No inference of novelty is drawn merely from its listing as a question.

The target uses every multilinear/outer commutator bracketing and one fixed prime p. Both x and y are single w-values, with p not dividing the order of x and p dividing the order of y; y need not have p-power order. The conclusion is that w(G) has a normal p-complement. The reverse implication is already discussed in the [original author manuscript](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2105.14474); novelty concerns the unrestricted forward direction.

The paper is Yerko Contreras Rojas, Valentina Grazian and Carmine Monetta, *p-nilpotency criteria for some verbal subgroups*, Journal of Algebra **609** (2022), 926–936, [DOI 10.1016/j.jalgebra.2022.07.017](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jalgebra.2022.07.017), [arXiv:2105.14474](https://arxiv.org/abs/2105.14474). Fresh inspection of the definition, Question 1 and Theorems C–D confirms the previously recorded scope: lower-central words for all finite groups, derived words for soluble finite groups. These do not cover the whole target.
