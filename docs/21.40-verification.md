## Exact formal scope

The unconditional endpoint is `Kourovka.P21_40.notebookStatement` in
[Solution.lean](../Kourovka/Problems/P21_40/Solution.lean). It proves that every
subgroup of `GL_n(ℚ)` with finitely many full abstract automorphism orbits
is virtually soluble, without a finite-generation hypothesis.

The stronger `structural_theorem` supplies one normal subgroup `K` with:

- Actual finite index and `[G:K] ≤ (2n+1)^(n²)`.
- One rational invertible matrix simultaneously conjugating all of `K`
  into the upper unitriangular group.
- Nilpotence of class at most `n−1`, using natural subtraction at `n=0`.
- No nonidentity element of finite order.

The unconditional `numberField_structure` extends the finite-index,
torsion-free nilpotent conclusion to arbitrary number fields by faithful
restriction of scalars. Its public statement has no rational-theorem
assumption; the internal bridge lemma's parameter is discharged in Lean.

The [independent statement audit](21.40-statement-audit.md) pins its reviewed
sources and checks the intended group action, finite-index convention,
quantifier order, nilpotency and torsion definitions, dimension zero,
and number-field transport. Separate component reviews covered the
arithmetic, trace-kernel, and finite-dimensional algebra arguments.
