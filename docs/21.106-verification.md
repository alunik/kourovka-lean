## Exact formal scope

The endpoint is `Kourovka.P21_106.not_notebookStatement` in
[Solution.lean](../Kourovka/Problems/P21_106/Solution.lean).
It unconditionally refutes conciseness of all parameter-free one-variable
first-order formulae in residually finite groups.

The formal statement and realization were reviewed against the mathematical
argument. In particular:

- The language has only the group operations and identity, with no named
  group elements or additional predicates.
- `counterexampleFormula` is a mathlib first-order syntax object with one
  free-variable index and prefix `exists a,b; forall g,h; exists u,v`.
- The realization theorem proves its equivalence to the algebraic condition.
- Finiteness concerns the entire formula-value set; infinitude concerns
  the carrier of its generated `Subgroup.closure`.
- Residual finiteness uses mathlib's standard `Group.ResiduallyFinite`,
  proved from the explicit finite coordinate quotients.
- The universal public question has no extra nilpotency, torsion,
  generation, or counterexample-specific assumptions.

The Lean proof verifies the properties needed for the full negative answer.
It does not additionally classify the whole centre or formalize every
strengthening in the informal mathematical note, such as the uniform
characterization over all commutative rings. These are not assumptions of
the endpoint.
