# Research and credit

[Repository](../README.md) · [Results](nilradical-v0.md) · [Credit corrections](credit-corrections.md)

This policy concerns Nilradical's contribution to Problems **21.3 (first
question), 21.38, 21.40, 21.44, 21.68 and 21.106**, credited to Nilradical v0.
It does not change the authorship or terms of other work in this repository.

## Use the results. Develop the mathematics.

You are welcome to use these results, develop new proofs and publish your
work. You do not need permission from Nilradical, and neither the agent nor
its developer or project team needs to be added as a coauthor.

Credit for a publication should go to the people who do the mathematical
work: understanding the ideas, checking the arguments, finding connections
and writing the paper. Citing an earlier discovery acknowledges its source;
it does not make its discoverer a coauthor of your publication.

A proof built for Lean is not necessarily the most elegant proof.
Formalization can favor a longer, self-contained argument or a route through
results that can themselves be formalized. A shorter proof, a clearer
explanation or a new connection is a valuable contribution.

Please cite the specific Nilradical result you use, together with the prior
work and people your paper builds on. Keep clear which results are already
known and which contributions are your own. The [result record](nilradical-v0.md)
provides citation instructions and exact source revisions.

This research policy does not replace licenses governing source code,
documents or third-party material. Existing license terms and attribution
requirements remain in force.

## Help us give credit accurately

Our attribution records begin with the agent's research. They can miss
contributors, prior results or an earlier solution. If we have omitted or
misstated credit, please email [research@nilradical.ai](mailto:research@nilradical.ai)
with the result and supporting sources. Research enquiries are welcome at the
same address. A [public GitHub reporting option](credit-corrections.md) is also
available.

Private reports do not require a public issue. We will not publish your
identity or private correspondence without your permission.

A human will review the report. Substantiated corrections will be dated,
linked to their evidence and reflected in the website and repository.
The [correction record](credit-corrections.md) preserves the explanation;
previously published snapshots and verification receipts remain available.
