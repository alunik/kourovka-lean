## Scope and source snapshot

The target is the complete negative answer to I. B. Gorshkov's Problem 21.53: for a finite nonabelian simple group and a whole involution conjugacy class, preservation of the exact product orders 2 and the least odd prime divisor need not force preservation of every exact product order. The final route uses Wilson's matrices over the eight-element field, proves the needed group properties directly, and exchanges two class members with identical centralizers. The order-three relation is empty, while the transposition changes a product order from 5 to 7.

The following is the reviewer's mathematical comparison, rather than a theorem asserted by those sources. Nonbinarity gives tuples whose pairs can be matched by group elements but whose full tuples cannot. Although the induced map on the tuple entries can be extended as an arbitrary set permutation, such an extension need not preserve either requested relation on pairs involving the remaining class members. Moreover, a global permutation preserving every orbital of the conjugation action would preserve every product-order color. Thus nonbinarity and orbital information alone do not supply the specific two-color versus all-color discrepancy needed for 21.53.

The previous broader inspections of Suzuki matrices, the twin-vertex principle, small-Ree graphs, field-two exceptional graphs and axial-algebra graph colors are retained in the 16 September dossier. They were not all repeated in this short release refresh; the present report does not claim otherwise.
