# Kourovka 21.35: references and credits

Companion bibliography and acknowledgments to [the mathematical note](21.35.md).

## The problem and mathematical sources

1. **Yerko Contreras Rojas, Valentina Grazian and Carmine Monetta**, Problem 21.35 in *The Kourovka Notebook*, 21st issue (2026), edited by **E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov**, printed p. 172. [Versioned source](https://arxiv.org/abs/1401.0300v46). Credit for proposing the problem belongs to the three proposers; credit for the collection belongs to its editors and contributors.

2. **Yerko Contreras Rojas, Valentina Grazian and Carmine Monetta**, *p-nilpotency criteria for some verbal subgroups*, Journal of Algebra **609** (2022), 926–936. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jalgebra.2022.07.017); [author preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2105.14474v1). Theorems C and D establish the lower-central and soluble derived-word cases. Their Lemmas 5.1 and 5.2 give the centralization mechanism used in the argument.

3. **John G. Thompson**, *Nonsolvable finite groups all of whose local subgroups are solvable*, Bulletin of the American Mathematical Society **74** (1968), 383–437. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1090/S0002-9904-1968-11953-6). The N-group work continued through [part VI](https://msp.org/pjm/1974/51-2/pjm-v51-n2-p30-s.pdf), Pacific Journal of Mathematics **51** (1974), 573–630. This is the source of the minimal-simple classification.

4. **Martin W. Liebeck, Eamonn A. O'Brien, Aner Shalev and Pham Huu Tiep**, *Commutators in finite quasisimple groups*, Bulletin of the London Mathematical Society **43** (2011), 1079–1092. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1112/blms/bdr043); [author manuscript](https://www.ma.imperial.ac.uk/~mwl/ORE-PLUS2.PDF). Theorem 1 and Table 1 imply the center-coprime commutator assertion used in the argument. Their paper in turn credits **Harvey I. Blau**, [*A fixed-point theorem for central elements in quasisimple groups*](https://doi.org/10.1090/S0002-9939-1994-1254833-X), Proceedings of the American Mathematical Society **122** (1994), 79–84, for the earlier classification of central exceptions.

5. **Martin W. Liebeck, Eamonn A. O'Brien, Aner Shalev and Pham Huu Tiep**, *The Ore conjecture*, Journal of the European Mathematical Society **12** (2010), 939–1008. [Publisher article](https://ems.press/journals/jems/articles/3979). **Øystein Ore** proposed the conjecture; these four authors proved it. The simple-group assertion also follows from the quasisimple result in item 4.

6. **Ferdinand Georg Frobenius**, normal p-complement criterion. For an exposition, see **I. Martin Isaacs**, *Finite Group Theory*, Graduate Studies in Mathematics **92**, American Mathematical Society, 2008, §5E, Theorem 5.26. [Publisher bibliographic pages](https://www.ams.org/books/gsm/092/gsm092-endmatter.pdf). The criterion is due to Frobenius; the exposition followed is Isaacs's.

7. **Josean da Silva Alves and Pavel Shumyatsky**, *On nilpotency of higher commutator subgroups of a finite soluble group*, Archiv der Mathematik **116** (2021), 1–6. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00013-020-01514-8); [author manuscript](https://arxiv.org/abs/2005.07579). Source for commutator-closed generation and quotient-lifting arguments.

8. **Cristina Acciarri, Gustavo A. Fernández-Alcober and Pavel Shumyatsky**, *A focal subgroup theorem for outer commutator words*, Journal of Group Theory **15** (2012), 397–405. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1515/jgt.2011.113); [author manuscript](https://arxiv.org/abs/1108.2284). This is the earlier source of the lifting lemmas reused by Alves–Shumyatsky. Its general focal theorem concerns powers of word values.

9. **Eloisa Detomi, Marta Morigi and Pavel Shumyatsky**, *On the rank of a verbal subgroup of a finite group*, Journal of the Australian Mathematical Society **113** (2022), 145–159. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1017/S1446788721000069); [author manuscript](https://arxiv.org/abs/2103.04102). The word-section argument uses the all-leaf case of Lemma 2.7, which credits the next item.

10. **Gustavo A. Fernández-Alcober and Marta Morigi**, *Outer commutator words are uniformly concise*, Journal of the London Mathematical Society **82** (2010), 581–595. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1112/jlms/jdq047); [author manuscript](https://arxiv.org/abs/0911.3048). Source of the outer-word tree and section methods, including Lemma 3.2 cited by Detomi–Morigi–Shumyatsky.

11. **Rolf Brandl**, *Groups with abelian Sylow subgroups*, Journal of the Australian Mathematical Society **88** (2010), 43–47. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1017/S1446788709000184). Source of the explicit split-torus construction in Lemma 4(c).

12. **Robert Steinberg**, [*Representations of Algebraic Groups*](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/nagoya-mathematical-journal/article/representations-of-algebraic-groups/02AE5498AF62BCAB6B313C75016E6015), Nagoya Mathematical Journal **22** (1963), 33–56. The characteristic-two SL₂ tensor description is a concrete instance of this classical representation theory.

These are identified mathematical sources for the problem, its previously established cases, and the results and constructions used here.
