# Problem 21.44 verification

[Problem overview](../Kourovka/Problems/P21_44/README.md) · [Documentation](README.md)

## Result and scope

The closed theorem `Kourovka.P21_44.notebookStatement` proves that the full
inverse limit of the iterated natural degree-five alternating wreath
products contains a finitely generated dense subgroup of subexponential
growth. The witness is the ordinary subgroup generated by the two explicit
compatible automorphisms. Its actual symmetric word balls satisfy
`log |B(n)| / n → 0`.

The [separate semantic review](21.44-statement-audit.md) checks the ambient
group, topology, quantifiers, and the complete discharge of the density and
growth hypotheses. The review was performed by a separately tasked Codex
agent; it is not outside human peer review. Integration changed only module
paths and the problem namespace, as checked in the
[integration manifest](21.44-statement-audit.json).

## Proof assumptions and finite computations

The public existence theorem has no parameters or unproved mathematical
hypotheses. Its transitive axiom closure is checked against the permitted
foundations `propext`, `Classical.choice`, and `Quot.sound` by
[Audit.lean](../Audit.lean).

The proof supplies the full finite-level generation argument, the sixteen
five-letter shortenings, the disjoint-block bound, the sparse-word encoding,
and the numerical growth argument. The finite checks use ordinary Lean
kernel computation. External Python checks are supplementary and are not
proof assumptions. The [finite-certificate guide](../Kourovka/Problems/P21_44/Proof/Certificates/README.md)
explains how to reproduce the sixty root-generation words independently.

Two general word-growth modules are reused under Apache-2.0. Their exact
source revision, original and adapted hashes, preserved attribution, and
import-only modification are recorded in the
[provenance guide](../Kourovka/Problems/P21_44/Proof/THIRD_PARTY.md).
