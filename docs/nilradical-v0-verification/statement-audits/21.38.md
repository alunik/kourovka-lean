## Source comparison and exact claim

The Notebook asks for a group whose ordinary spread is exactly one. Donoven and Harper, Infinite 3/2-generated groups, Question 2, ask explicitly for an infinite such group. The original Question 2 was checked directly at https://arxiv.org/html/1907.05498v2#S5 on 16 September 2026.

There is an infinite group G such that every nonidentity x in G has y in G generating G with x, while some fixed pair of nonidentity elements a,b has no common generating companion. The concrete equal-endpoint subgroup satisfies the tuple condition exactly for k <= 1.

The [Notebook v46](https://arxiv.org/abs/1401.0300v46), printed page 173, was compared directly with its local PDF text (physical PDF page 173; local extraction (retained privately; [source PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1401.0300v46)), lines 8208–8216). Source SHA-256: `498c2443bcee6ca398430935c87c97b6f99d0b1a6e5cb8f637d188a1827115da`.

The reviewed website summary and scope are preserved in [the mathematical-scope snapshot](sources/reviewed-site-mathematical-scope.json). They match the formal scope:

> An explicit subgroup of Thompson’s group F is infinite and has ordinary spread exactly one.
>
> The subgroup consists of maps with equal endpoint slopes; the spread condition holds exactly for k ≤ 1. The key generation argument specializes Gili Golan-Polak’s published theorem.

## Definition, quantifier, and witness checks

1. GeneratesPair uses ordinary Subgroup.closure of {a,b} inside G. It is neither normal closure nor topological closure. HasSpreadAtLeast quantifies over every Fin k tuple of nonidentity elements, without demanding distinct entries or prescribing a conjugacy class for y.

2. HasSpreadExactlyOne is the one-tuple property together with failure of the two-tuple property. SpreadBounds proves downward monotonicity and equivalence to the full profile k <= 1, so this is the source maximum convention, not an ad hoc weaker condition.

3. The witness F is the subtype compactF 0 1 of actual permutations of Q. PLGroup requires strict monotonicity and grid-affinity of both the map and inverse; powSlopes 0 gives precisely integer powers of 2. The interval group fixes all points outside (0,1). On [0,1] a uniform dyadic grid gives finitely many affine pieces.

4. The left and right endpoint exponents are proved to exist and be unique from actual affine germ equations. diagonalSubgroup requires equality of those exponents. Generation is not a field in the definition of this group.

5. exists_generating_companion assumes only f != 1. It obtains GenerationData from the explicit companion construction and proves CoreLocalInterpolation from the data. This includes f in the endpoint kernel: no nonzero slope assumption is present. DiagonalGeneration then proves core containment, cancels endpoint characters, and fills the quotient with integer powers of the companion.

6. The upper bound chooses two actual nonidentity compact-core elements before quantifying over every companion. Equal endpoint exponents force each companion to fix a rational interior point; one member of the pair fixes that point, obstructing generation. Infinitude is independently proved by an injective sequence of powers of a concrete increasing permutation.

7. Both existential endpoints are closed. Infinite G is explicitly included in OriginalQuestion. No model-supplied generation, interpolation, or infinitude hypothesis survives in the public answer.
